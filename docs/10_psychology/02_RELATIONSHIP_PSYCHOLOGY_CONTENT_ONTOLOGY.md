# Relationship Psychology Content Ontology (RPCO) v1.0

**Status:** Canonical draft for Psychology Sub-Constitution integration  
**Canonical machine-readable source:** YAML file

## Purpose

Canonical map Hermes uses to plan, classify, generate, track and learn from the husband-wife relationship and emotional psychology channel without inventing random topics.

## Hierarchy

- L1 Niche: Psychology
- L2 Domain: Relationship & Emotional Psychology
- L3 Problem Family
- L4 Sub-Series / Mechanism Family
- L5 Mechanism Variant
- L6 Story Universe
- L7 Story Arc
- L8 Episode or Multi-Part Episode
- L9 Distribution Assets

## Critical separation

**RPCO is independent of Mark & Sarah.** It defines the psychology territory. Mark & Sarah are the current flagship story universe that dramatizes that territory.

## Episode Laws

- Exactly one primary psychological mechanism per episode.
- Secondary mechanisms may support but must not compete with the primary mechanism.
- Responsibility rotates realistically: Mark, Sarah, both, or circumstances.
- Never permanently cast one spouse as the flawed partner and the other as the wiser rescuer.
- Problems must remain plausible in ordinary married life and inside the approved safe scope.
- Every episode moves toward constructive understanding, repair, healing, meaningful progress, or a greater-good conclusion.
- Multi-part stories require the complete causal arc and intended final resolution before Part 1 is produced.
- Temporary personal space may be used constructively; permanent Mark/Sarah separation is prohibited.
- No diagnosis, therapy impersonation, medical advice, or scientific/clinical authority.
- Explain what is happening beneath the silence through story first; use concise explicit interpretation only when useful.

## Hermes Planning Algorithm

1. Read Psychology Sub-Constitution + RPCO before proposing content.
2. Inspect produced/planned inventory; never select topics randomly.
3. Prefer an under-covered high-priority Problem Family or Sub-Series unless ORACLE evidence justifies another choice.
4. Select exactly one primary mechanism and state its viewer-recognition value.
5. Check Mark & Sarah continuity and avoid repeating the same causal situation, conflict choreography, or resolution.
6. Choose a believable initiating life context from the established world.
7. Rotate responsibility patterns across the catalog.
8. Define the complete causal arc and constructive resolution before screenplay generation.
9. Choose single-part vs multi-part based on story integrity, never only engagement tactics.
10. Create an Episode Contract and send it through GENESIS quality gates.
11. Only after narrative approval may PROMETHEUS create production assets.
12. Generate the canonical 16:9 master first and derive platform assets from it.
13. After publishing, ORACLE updates performance by Problem Family, Sub-Series, mechanism variant, hook, format and resolution.
14. Use accumulated evidence to guide coverage without rewriting constitutional boundaries or becoming formulaic.

## Why the mutable coverage registry is separate

RPCO is the stable map. Episode counts, publication state, CTR, retention, lessons, and ORACLE findings are mutable operational data. Hermes should update a separate coverage registry rather than changing the ontology whenever a video is produced.
