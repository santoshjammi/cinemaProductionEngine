# GENESIS Execution Contract

**Version:** 1.0

## Input

Required:

- approved Episode Contract;
- Effective Policy Snapshot;
- selected RPCO nodes;
- story-universe canon;
- relevant continuity;
- applicable standards;
- Format Profile.

## Output

GENESIS must produce creative knowledge sufficient for deterministic production:

- narrative thesis;
- logline;
- synopsis;
- causal beat graph;
- screenplay;
- exact dialogue;
- dialogue intent/subtext/delivery;
- scene emotional states;
- continuity implications;
- visual bible;
- shot plan;
- voice direction;
- sound/music intent;
- asset requirements;
- executable PKP.

## Prohibited

GENESIS does not produce:

- final images;
- final TTS audio;
- final lip-sync media;
- final music files;
- final video;
- final master.

## Quality gate

GENESIS output must be reviewed against:

- Psychology Constitution;
- Series Constitution;
- Dialogue Standard;
- Emotional Continuity Standard;
- Anti-AI-Slop Standard;
- production feasibility.

Only then freeze PKP.
