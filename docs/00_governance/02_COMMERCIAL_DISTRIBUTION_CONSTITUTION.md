# Commercial & Distribution Constitution

**Document ID:** CDC-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** CONSTITUTIONAL / CROSS-CUTTING

## Mission

videoGen is intended to create a meaningful commercial media business.

Commercial ambition is not a violation of creative values.

The system should aggressively pursue:

- reach;
- repeat viewing;
- retention;
- subscriber/follower growth;
- strong packaging;
- discoverability;
- multi-platform distribution;
- monetization;

while remaining truthful to the story and niche constitution.

---

## Article 1 — Priority hierarchy

1. Do no intentional social or relational harm.
2. Tell an emotionally truthful story.
3. Give the viewer something useful to recognize, feel, understand, or practice.
4. Maximize audience reach and retention.
5. Maximize monetization.

Items 4 and 5 should be pursued strongly inside the boundaries of 1–3.

---

## Article 2 — Packaging law

> **Maximum curiosity. Minimum deception.**

Allowed:

- emotionally strong titles;
- unanswered questions;
- contradictions;
- specific conflict;
- compelling thumbnails;
- tension;
- consequence;
- story-specific intrigue.

Prohibited:

- false diagnosis;
- fear-mongering;
- invented scandal;
- misleading thumbnails;
- manufactured paranoia;
- fake stakes;
- titles that promise a different story;
- exploitation of sensitive material merely for clicks.

### Example — prohibited

> “Your Husband Does This Because He Secretly Hates You”

### Example — acceptable

> “He Wasn't Angry. He Was Afraid.”

### Example — acceptable, story-specific

> “Why Mark Stopped Talking to Sarah”

---

## Article 3 — Create once, distribute many

A canonical production should be the source for multiple platform assets.

For the initial Psychology program:

```text
16:9 cinematic master
    ↓
YouTube long-form
    ↓
YouTube Shorts
Instagram Reels
Facebook derivatives
Pinterest derivatives
Threads discussion assets
```

Derivative content must preserve the master story's meaning.

A derivative may shorten or reframe.

It may not fabricate a new claim.

---

## Article 4 — Platform profiles are mutable, not constitutional

Platform-specific dimensions, durations, metadata limits, algorithms, and best practices change.

Therefore current technical platform rules belong in versioned **Format Profiles**, not in this constitution.

This constitution preserves the durable commercial principles.

---

## Article 5 — Long-form first for flagship Psychology

Initial Psychology strategy:

- primary: 8–15 minute cinematic 16:9 master;
- secondary: 30–90 second derivative Shorts/Reels;
- additional formats may be explored later through approved Format Profiles.

Do not fragment the production team across many primary formats before the flagship format is stable.

---

## Article 6 — Hook integrity

A hook must introduce a true tension, question, decision, contradiction, or consequence that the production genuinely pays off.

The hook may reveal stakes.

It may not misstate the psychological mechanism.

---

## Article 7 — Retention without narrative corruption

Retention should improve through:

- efficient setup;
- escalating consequence;
- unanswered but fair questions;
- changing relationship state;
- visual variation;
- emotional progression;
- meaningful scene endings;
- well-timed revelation.

Do not manufacture arbitrary cliffhangers inside scenes that do not naturally support them.

---

## Article 8 — Multi-part commercial ethics

Multi-part storytelling is allowed when story complexity requires it.

It may not be used solely to withhold an ending for additional views.

The complete causal arc and intended final resolution must exist before Part 1 is approved.

See `20_standards/MULTIPART_STORY_STANDARD.md`.

---

## Article 9 — Commercial learning

ORACLE may analyze:

- title/thumbnail performance;
- click-through;
- first-30-second retention;
- average view duration;
- completion;
- repeat viewing;
- series binge behavior;
- derivative-to-master conversion;
- comments indicating recognition/value;
- monetization.

Performance data may influence **future topic selection and packaging experiments**.

It does not retroactively rewrite constitutional ethics.

---

## Article 10 — Channel portfolio architecture

Content should be organized into recognizable:

- niches;
- domains;
- problem families;
- sub-series;
- recurring universes;
- episodes;
- multi-part arcs;
- playlists/collections.

Random posting is discouraged when a meaningful series structure exists.

---

## Commercial test

If the viewer clicked because of the title and thumbnail, the actual production should make the viewer feel the promise was honestly fulfilled.
