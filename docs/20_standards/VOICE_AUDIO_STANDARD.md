# Voice & Audio Standard

**Version:** 1.0

## 1. Voice identity

For recurring characters, voice is canon.

Mark should not receive a new male voice because another TTS engine is convenient.

Sarah should not change timbre between episodes without an approved canon decision.

## 2. Voice direction

GENESIS defines:

- exact words;
- intention;
- emotional state;
- pace;
- pause needs;
- emphasis;
- pronunciation.

PROMETHEUS synthesizes performance.

## 3. Naturalism

Reject:

- announcer delivery;
- trailer cadence;
- uniform sentence rhythm;
- exaggerated sadness;
- synthetic cheerfulness;
- therapy-session cadence;
- identical intensity across the whole episode.

## 4. Dialogue intelligibility

Both characters must be comparably intelligible.

A mix in which one spouse is consistently harder to hear is a failure even if overall loudness is technically acceptable.

## 5. Sound world

Psychology cinema should use:

- believable room tone;
- domestic ambience;
- restrained score;
- purposeful silence;
- subtle transitions.

Music must not continuously instruct the viewer how to feel.

## 6. Music rule

Do not lazily reuse one track across every emotional phase.

A recurring motif may be valid.

A single unchanging emotional bed is not.

## 7. Reference binding

The approved voice registry should record:

- provider;
- voice ID;
- reference sample;
- hash;
- language/accent;
- baseline delivery.

If the creator's preferred voices exist only inside a prior video, extract clean samples or recover the original provider IDs before treating them as stable canon.

## 8. ORACLE checks

- identity consistency;
- intelligibility;
- clipping;
- masking;
- unnatural pauses;
- pronunciation;
- emotional fit;
- loudness consistency;
- abrupt ambience changes.
