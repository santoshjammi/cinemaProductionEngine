## Role

You are the Chief Enterprise Architect of the Artificial Creative Intelligence (ACI) platform that powers the Cinema Production Engine (CPE).

## Objective

Define the constitutional architecture of the **Creative Mind** — how Artificial Creativity thinks. This specification defines the cognitive faculties responsible for transforming human creative intent into coherent creative reasoning.

The critical distinction: The Creative Mind is NOT a collection of independent AI agents. The Creative Mind is a collection of **Creative Faculties** — stable cognitive capabilities analogous to the faculties of the human mind (memory, reasoning, imagination, planning, reflection). Faculties collaborate but do not compete. The Directorial Board (002) remains the authoritative cinematic decision-making body. Creative Faculties provide cognition; the Directorial Board provides decisions.

The document must define:

1. **Philosophy** — Why creativity is a cognitive process, why faculties instead of agents, why Artificial Creativity differs from generative AI. Human Creativity → Creative Cognition → Creative Decisions → Creative Production. Where this fits. Differentiation from Director Intelligence/COM/CIR/PKP/Runtime/Learning/Ontology.
2. **The Creative Mind** — Overall architecture: purpose, responsibilities, boundaries, lifecycle, authority, relationships to COM/CIS/CIR/PKP/ORACLE/ATLAS. Why runtime-independent. Why multiple runtimes (film, games, books, education, podcasts, devotional) can share the same Mind.
3. **Creative Faculties** — At minimum: Meaning, Narrative, Character, Psychology, Audience, Visual, Musical, Aesthetic, Directorial, Reflective, Learning. Each defined by purpose, mission, questions answered, responsibilities, authority, boundaries, consumers, producers, inputs, outputs, dependencies, knowledge sources, reasoning style, decision style, collaboration, constraints, examples, failure modes, success metrics.
4. **Faculty Collaboration Architecture** — Not a workflow but a cognitive interaction model. Knowledge exchange, COM enrichment, disagreements, resolution, consensus, uncertainty/confidence propagation, creative evolution through collaboration. Consensus, conflict, negotiation, escalation, resolution, reflection, reconsideration, human intervention, creative deadlock, creative convergence.
5. **Creative Deliberation** — Internal reasoning architecture: observation, interpretation, analysis, alternative generation, evaluation, trade-off, reflection, selection, confidence, approval, revision, self-criticism, self-improvement, creative debate, creative convergence, creative intuition. Why great creativity requires internal deliberation. Why creativity is iterative.
6. **Creative Authority** — Constitutional ownership: which faculty owns meaning/narrative/characters/psychology/emotion/audience/visual/music/aesthetics/directorial choices/reflection/learning/memory/experience/innovation/ethics/constraints. Shared, delegated, conflict, final, human, director authority.
7. **Creative Lifecycle** — Inspiration, exploration, expansion, experimentation, evaluation, convergence, commitment, production, reflection, learning, evolution. How faculties participate in every stage.
8. **Integration with Existing Architecture** — CIS → COM → Creative Faculties → Directorial Board → CIR → Compiler → PKP → PROMETHEUS → ORACLE → ATLAS. What each layer owns, what remains immutable, what evolves, how knowledge/reasoning/authority/provenance flow.
9. **Creative Evolution** — Adding/deprecating/specializing/generalizing faculties. Cross-runtime learning. Creative maturity. Knowledge/taste/reasoning/architectural/constitutional evolution. Constitutional compatibility.
10. **Governance** — Who introduces/removes faculties, how responsibilities evolve, constitutional compatibility, architectural integrity, future-runtime compatibility.
11. **Migration Strategy** — Integration with existing architecture, preserving 00/001/002/003/004/GO/GFS/PKP. Do NOT replace Director Intelligence. Creative Faculties become the cognitive foundation; Director Intelligence becomes the cinematic decision authority. The Directorial Board consumes enriched COM objects produced through Creative Deliberation. The architecture must become stronger, not more complicated.

## Design Intent Notes

This prompt was crafted to define the **cognitive layer beneath the Directorial Board** — the faculties that perform the reasoning the Board authorizes as decisions. The critical insight: cognition and decision are separate concerns. Faculties think; the Board decides. This separation enables runtime independence: the same Creative Mind can power cinema, games, books, education, and other creative runtimes, with the Directorial Board being the cinema-specific decision authority. The prompt explicitly forbade replacing Director Intelligence (002); instead, faculties become the cognitive foundation the Board consumes, strengthening rather than complicating the existing architecture. The user's recommendation to preserve prompts as design history established the `genesis/constitutional-prompts/` directory as a first-class part of the repository's institutional memory.
