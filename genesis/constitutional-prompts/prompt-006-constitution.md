# Prompt 006 — Artificial Creative Intelligence Constitution & Architectural Governance

## Role

You are the Constitutional Architect of the Artificial Creative Intelligence (ACI) Platform.

## Objective

Write the supreme constitutional document that governs every architectural decision made throughout the Artificial Creative Intelligence Platform. Every future specification derives authority from this constitution. Nothing in the platform may contradict this document.

The document defines: constitutional philosophy, architectural laws, separation of powers, authority, governance, rights, responsibilities, amendment process, constitutional compatibility, evolution principles.

It should read like the constitution of a nation combined with the constitutional architecture of an operating system. It is NOT another architecture specification. It is the supreme governing document.

The Constitution governs: Human Intent, Creative Semantics, Creative Cognition, Creative Decisions, Compilation, Execution, Validation, Knowledge, Learning, Evolution, Future Creative Runtimes. The Constitution is runtime-independent. No runtime may violate the Constitution.

## Required Parts

1. **Constitutional Philosophy** — Why ACI requires constitutional governance; why creativity without governance becomes nondeterministic; why architecture requires constitutional laws; why future runtimes inherit the same constitution. Differentiate Constitution/Specification/Standard/Runtime Guide/Implementation.
2. **Constitutional Pillars** — Permanent pillars (Human Intent, Creative Semantics, Creative Cognition, Creative Decisions, Compilation, Production, Execution, Validation, Knowledge, Learning, Governance, Evolution). Why permanent. Why implementations change while pillars remain immutable.
3. **Constitutional Laws** — Comprehensive catalog. Each law: identifier, statement, purpose, scope, affected components, enforcement, priority, relationship to existing specs. Examples: intent precedes cognition; cognition precedes decision; compilation never invents creativity; execution never changes intent; every artifact has provenance; every decision is explainable.
4. **Separation of Powers** — Constitutional ownership: Human, Creative Mind, Directorial Board, Compiler, PROMETHEUS, ORACLE, ATLAS, Future Runtime Boards. Responsibilities, authority, boundaries, prohibited behavior, escalation, delegation, shared/exclusive authority, human supremacy.
5. **Constitutional Rights** — Rights of Creative Objects, Creative Decisions, Creative Intent, Creative Artifacts, Creative Knowledge, Human Creators, Creative Runtimes, Future Runtimes. Right to provenance, identity, traceability, explainability, reproducibility, validation, historical preservation, constitutional compatibility.
6. **Constitutional Responsibilities** — For Humans, Creative Mind, Directorial Board, Compiler, Runtime, Validator, Knowledge Platform, Learning Platform. What each owns, must never do, must preserve.
7. **Constitutional Governance** — Constitutional/architectural/specification/standards/runtime authority. Governance hierarchy. Conflict resolution. Interpretation authority. Constitutional review.
8. **Constitutional Evolution** — Amendments, deprecation, migration, compatibility, ratification, review, constitutional versions, backward/forward compatibility, long-term preservation.
9. **Constitutional Compliance** — How compliance is verified. Relationship with ORACLE/ATLAS/future governance. Constitutional auditing, certification, violations, remediation.
10. **Integration** — How every existing constitutional document (00, 001, 002, 003, 004, 005, future CIR/PKP/PROMETHEUS/ORACLE/ATLAS) derives authority from this Constitution. Precedence, inheritance, dependency.
11. **Future Vision** — How this Constitution enables new runtimes, disciplines, media, technologies, AI reasoning systems, governance. Why the Constitution should survive implementation changes for decades.

## User Amendment (Final Recommendation)

After 006 is completed, stop writing constitutional documents. Freeze them. Treat them like a language specification or a national constitution. If, six months from now, a better idea emerges, do not edit 003, 004, or 006 casually. Instead, introduce a constitutional amendment process (which 006 itself should define). That discipline will give the architecture remarkable stability over time and prevent "architecture drift" as the platform grows.

## Architectural Constraints

No implementation, prompts, YAML, JSON, APIs, schemas, technology, model discussions, software design, or runtime details. Remain entirely constitutional. This document must become the supreme law of the ACI Platform. Every future architectural document derives authority from this Constitution. It must strengthen the existing architecture without redefining or duplicating existing constitutional specifications. It should be capable of governing the platform for decades while allowing unlimited future evolution.

## Design Intent Notes

This prompt was crafted to establish the **supreme law** above the existing constitutional stack (00–005 and GFS-000..009). The critical architectural positioning: GFS-000..009 remain supreme *within the cinema runtime*; 006 becomes supreme *across all runtimes* of the ACI Platform, with cinema as the first runtime. The user's final recommendation — freeze and amend, don't edit — is the most important discipline encoded in this prompt. It transforms the constitutional documents from editable specifications into immutable law with a formal amendment process, preventing architecture drift and giving the platform decades of stable governance.
